# Documents

**Project → Documents** controls the look of the PDFs your store generates — invoices, receipts and similar paperwork.

## What you can do

- **Pick a template** — choose a style (e.g. modern, classic, minimal) with a live preview.
- **Add your company details** — name, address, registration / tax numbers.
- **Brand it** — upload a logo and pick an accent color.
- **Set an email footer / signature** that goes out with documents.

Every field saves as you change it, with a small confirmation toast — there's no Save button.

The result is applied automatically to documents customers receive (e.g. an order's invoice), so your paperwork matches your brand.
