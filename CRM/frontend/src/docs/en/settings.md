# Settings

**Project → Settings** holds the project-wide preferences that shape how the rest of the console behaves. Everything saves as you change it.

## General

- **Timezone** — pick from presets or set a custom one. It's used wherever dates and times are shown, and seeds booking slot times.
- **Currency** — the symbol and formatting used for every price across the dashboard. Past orders keep the currency they were placed in, so historical reports stay accurate when you change it.

## Inventory

- **Batch consumption** — FIFO or LIFO, used when stock is tracked in [batches](/docs/products).
- **Price display** — toggles like hiding prices or showing margins on the catalog.

## Barcode defaults

Choose what your generated barcodes encode (SKU, product id, batch, price).

## Project-level vs account-level

This page is per **project**. Your personal preferences (interface language, theme) live under **account settings**, and organization-wide options (billing, shared customers, team) live under the [organization](/docs/organizations-projects).
