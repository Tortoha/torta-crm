# Organizations & Projects

## Organizations

An **organization** is your top-level account. It holds your projects, your team, billing/payment settings, and organization-wide preferences.

Open **Organization → Settings** to rename it, set the display currency for org-level analytics, or manage the customer-sharing mode (below).

## Projects

A **project** is a single store (or branch). It owns its own:

- products, orders, returns, customers
- analytics, alerts, targets
- authentication providers (email, Google, phone, …)
- API keys

Switch between projects from the breadcrumb switcher at the top of the page.

## Team & roles

Invite teammates under **Organization → Team**. Each member gets a **role** per project, and roles control which pages they can see and edit (Products, Orders, Chat, and so on). The organization owner always has full access.

## Shared customers (multi-branch)

Under **Organization → Settings → Customers** you choose how customer accounts behave across the org's projects:

- **Shared across org** *(default)* — one customer account works at every branch. A customer signs up once and is recognized in every store. Authentication settings are shared across branches too. Best for chains and multi-branch brands.
- **Per project** — each store keeps its own isolated customer base. A customer of one branch is not a customer of another.

Switching to *Per project* asks you to type the organization name to confirm, because once branches diverge it can't always be merged back.
