# Emails

**Project → Emails** is where you control the email your store sends — both the automatic messages and one-off campaigns.

## Transactional templates

The emails your store sends on its own: account verification, password reset, order confirmation, booking reminder, abandoned cart, restock notification, and similar. For each you can:

- Edit the content in a **visual editor** or raw **HTML**.
- Insert **variables** like the customer's name or order id, which fill in per recipient.
- See a **live preview** and send yourself a **test**.
- Set the subject and from-name.

## Broadcasts

The broadcasts tab sends a custom message to a segment of your [customers](/docs/customers-page) — announcements, promos, newsletters.

## Delivery

Mail is sent through Torta's own email service. To send from your own domain (and improve deliverability), verify it under [Authentication](/docs/authentication) → Email, which walks you through the DNS records.
