# Project Overview

The landing page of a project's console (**Project → Overview**). It's your daily cockpit: a quick read on the store's health plus the keys you need to connect it.

## What you'll see

- **Key metrics** — at-a-glance tiles: today's orders, today's revenue, your plan, and whether email sign-in is set up.
- **Copy Keys** — the dropdown that reveals your project's **public**, **publishable** and **secret** keys. The secret key is shown to owners only. These connect your storefront — see [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart) and [Concepts](/docs/concepts).
- **Health checks** — flags gaps in auth, inventory, alerts and analytics, each with a link to fix it.
- **Needs attention** — action cards with live counts: orders to fulfil, return requests, upcoming bookings, restock requests, unread messages.
- **Store advisor** — actionable suggestions; quiet when everything's in order.
- **Recent activity** — a live feed of new orders and bookings with status badges.

## What you can do here

- Copy your API keys to wire up the storefront.
- Jump straight to whatever needs attention (fulfil an order, answer a message, restock).
- Rename the project from the breadcrumb switcher at the top.

Most tiles and cards are shortcuts — click them to go to the relevant page ([Orders](/docs/orders), [Booking](/docs/booking-page), [Chat](/docs/chat-page), …).
