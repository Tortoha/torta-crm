# Customers

**Project → Customers** is your address book and CRM record of everyone who has shopped or signed up.

## What you can do

- **Browse & search** by name, email or phone.
- **Sort** by recent activity, total spent, order count, name or join date.
- **Open a customer** to see their full profile: contact details, purchase history, payment breakdown, reviews and preferences.
- **Filter** by lifetime value, order count or signup date to find segments.

## Where customers come from

- Shoppers who sign up on your storefront through the SDK ([Authentication](/docs/auth)).
- Records you push from your own backend with the secret key — see [Pushing Customers](/docs/customers).

> Whether a customer is shared across all branches of an organization or kept per-project is set in **Organization → Settings** — see [Organizations & Projects](/docs/organizations-projects).

You can email customers or segments from the [Emails](/docs/emails) page.
